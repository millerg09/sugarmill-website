About the studio

Sugar Mill Music is a small, single-room studio built for songwriters who are early in their journey. It's not a big Brooklyn warehouse — it's one room, set up to be comfortable, quiet, and easy to work in, so you can focus on the song instead of the gear.

Services

  • Vocal recording

    Clean, guided vocal takes in a comfortable, low-pressure room.

  • Mixing

    Polished mixes that keep your song sounding like you.

  • Production & coaching

    Light production help and honest feedback for first-time artists.
