About the studio

Sugar Mill Music is a home recording studio in Upper Manhattan perfect for co-writing, vocal tracking, recording overdubs and polishing arrangements. A simple and focused one-room studio in a cozy, residential space.

Collection of gear includes a Peluso P-12 tube microphone, vintage Neve clone Pre73-Jr style preamps, UA Apollo Quad and Yamaha HS-7 monitors.

Instruments include acoustic and electric guitars, electric bass, banjo, violin, Nashville guitar, baritone guitar, Nord Electro 4 HP and mini drum kit.

Services

  • Mixing
